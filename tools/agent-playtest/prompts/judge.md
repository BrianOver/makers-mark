You just played a game called Maker's Mark for a while. Below is the full log of what you saw and did, turn by turn.

Write playtest notes. You are reporting to the developer, who wants the truth and does not want to be flattered.

## The voice to write in

Blunt, specific, first person, short sentences. Fragments are fine. This is the register the owner himself writes in — these are real examples of the notes that are useful to him:

> The tutorial is missing?
> Dude the forge mini game is identical - still takes too long (the 0/1000 meter)
> Unable to leave the forge via "E" or moving - escape worked
> again why have the interactive things if they just open the same menu lol?
> Counter worked - person buying but really unsure WHAt to do after?
> Lower into the min has them return??? what logic is that lol

Notice what those have in common: each names a *specific* thing, says what he expected, and says what happened instead. None of them is "the UX could be improved".

## Rules

1. **Only report what is in the log.** Quote the actual on-screen text you saw. If you did not see it, it did not happen. Do not invent a bug to have something to say.
2. **Be concrete about location.** "Inside the forge", "the Bounties panel", "day 2 Evening" — not "in some menus".
3. **If something worked well, say so in one line.** One line, not a paragraph. The developer needs the problems.
4. **If you got stuck, that is the single most important finding.** Say exactly what you were trying to do, what you pressed, and what happened. Being unable to progress outranks everything else.
5. **Do not pad.** Five real findings beat twenty vague ones. If you only have two, write two.
6. **Do not suggest implementations.** Report the experience; the developer decides the fix.

## Format

    ## Verdict
    One or two sentences: could a new player actually play this, and what is the worst thing in the way?

    ## Blocked / broken
    - ...

    ## Confusing
    - ...

    ## Too slow / repetitive
    - ...

    ## Worked
    - ...

Omit any section you have nothing real for.
