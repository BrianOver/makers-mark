MAKER'S MARK — DESIGN REVIEW & RECOMMENDATIONS
Date: 2026-08-06
Reviewed: the-game.md, mechanics-ground-truth.md, hero-facing-day.md,
all-building-minigames.md, composed-track-forensics.md

================================================================
OVERALL VERDICT
================================================================
The concept is strong and the execution discipline is unusually good.
The deterministic sim + counterfactual attribution engine is a genuine
moat: it makes "your actions matter" PROVABLE instead of flavor text.
The hero-facing-day reframing (fill the middle with the smith's hands,
not automate it away) is the right call. Most standard recommendations
the docs already caught themselves. What follows is what they haven't
fully said, plus rulings on the Section 9 open questions, since those
are the actual bottleneck.

================================================================
THE PATTERN THE DOCS CIRCLE BUT NEVER NAME
================================================================
The game's two most human verbs are both strictly dominated by doing
nothing:

  1. The counter (the face-to-face beat) is out-earned ~10x by the
     invisible atomic shopping pass (Sec 9.5).
  2. The camp supply run (the thesis-statement verb) measures
     +29 deaths and -12 target-reached (Sec 9.9).

That is not two separate tuning questions — it is one systemic defect:
the game teaches players to skip its humanity. The project's own
"bounty theater" law bans verbs that occupy hands without changing
outcomes; a verb that changes outcomes FOR THE WORSE is strictly worse
than theater.

PRINCIPLE TO ADOPT: Hand-work must beat passive systems, or the
passive system must not exist.

================================================================
RULINGS ON SECTION 9 OPEN QUESTIONS
================================================================

9.9 — PROVISIONING IRONY: DEFECT, RETUNE. NOT A TRAGEDY.
A tragedy requires a tradeoff. If provisioning bought depth at the
cost of blood, "your kindness emboldens them" would be a beautiful
theme. But the measurement says supply loses on BOTH axes — more
deaths AND fewer targets reached. That is not tragic irony; it is a
dominated verb. Take option (c): keep individual saves provable, damp
only the risk-compensation push (post-floor quaff no longer resets the
too-hurt check), and re-sweep until supply trades depth against
survival — a real dilemma with two goods. THEN surfacing "the
well-supplied dare too much" in gossip becomes honest. Do this before
more vigil presentation lands; #392 already built UI on a signal
pointing the wrong way.

9.5 — COUNTER: MAKE IT THE PREMIUM CHANNEL.
Personal service should out-earn the shelf (better pins, higher
willingness, goodwill compounding into commission premiums). Also fix
the honesty gap: the UI implies "push too hard and lose them" but a
counter-offer can never lose the sale. Either add real walk risk or
make the surface say what's true. Staged tension the sim doesn't cash
is the project's own named failure class.

9.10 — PHASE-D SINKS: SLOT INTO THE ORDER, AFTER H3.
The shipped client is still the "money with no meaning" game the
audits condemned. Three of four sinks already have bell-tray strings;
this is mostly buttons. Do not defer past v1 — a 5-6x gold pile with
nowhere to go undermines every economic decision the rest of the
design creates.

9.3 — FORGE DIFFICULTY: ASSIST THE BEGINNER.
19.5s with the beginner being exactly who complains is a first-session
killer, and first sessions decide whether anyone but the owner ever
plays this. Inverse session-skill (bigger shape-per-strike at low
demonstrated accuracy) preserves the covenant: labor shrinks, the
accuracy target never widens.

9.1 — EMBERFALL: ENTRYPOWER 79; PARK #346 EXPLICITLY WITH A NOTE.
A draft that implies "one click away" when the venue has zero art is
exactly the drift this project keeps getting burned by. Either
commission the art wave or park it in writing.

9.2 — LEGEND ENGINE: RETIRE THE MODULE, KEEP THE PROMISE.
Agree with the standing recommendation. If the feel-test says legends
read as logs, the fix is a composer/selector pass over existing data,
not the sifter machinery.

9.7 — THE FINAL COMMISSION IS OWED.
The game's thesis is "your craft writes the legends," and the current
climax is a threshold event with a tally. The ending that pays off the
premise is forging one named piece for one named hero to carry to the
Heart. Not before the loop wave — but the campaign is not "complete"
without it. Rule that now so nobody re-litigates it later.

9.6 — BLESS THE BUILD ORDER AS WRITTEN, with one insertion:
The 9.9 retune goes before H5 (stakes slate), and Phase-D surfaces go
after H3 (night leads with the mark).

9.8 — THE FEEL-TEST: SCHEDULE IT. (See below.)

9.4 — DEMAND-HAZARD ENGINE: KEEP AS NEXT BIG SIM PROGRAM after the
loop wave and the 9.9 retune. Two professions have the newest craft
surfaces with the least reason to use them; typed demand is what pays
them off. Nothing in the current margins supersedes it.

================================================================
TWO NOTES ON THE CORE CONCEPT ITSELF
================================================================

1. "COZY" NEEDS AN HONEST MODIFIER.
What is actually built is not Stardew-cozy — it is Spiritfarer-cozy:
warmth ABOUT LOSS. Permadeath is the only stake, Prepared heroes die
~63-66%, and the memorial -> heirloom -> lineage loop is the emotional
center. That is a stronger, more distinctive identity than generic
cozy — but own it deliberately in tone and (eventually) marketing.
Players arriving for Stardew will bounce off the graveyard; players
who would love this game will not find it under a "cozy sim" label.

2. THE BOTTLENECK IS NOW THE OWNER, NOT THE AGENTS.
The docs are exceptional — and 2,100+ lines of design writing in five
days while 9.8's human feel-test sits undone is its own signal.
Machine playtests have gone as far as they can; every remaining big
ruling (legends, ending, boredom day) is gated on one real evening at
the keyboard answering the five written questions:
  - Do four crafts feel like four skills?
  - Can you name three heroes by personality?
  - Does a legend read as a story you can retell?
  - Does the ending land?
  - On which day does it get boring?
That evening is worth more than the next three PRs. Schedule it before
ruling on anything downstream of it.

================================================================
AUDIO (COMPOSED-TRACK FORENSICS)
================================================================
Nothing to add beyond: hold the U9 acceptance bar the forensics pass
defines (raw LUFS ~ -21.7, no positive TrimDb, >=180s, no windowed RMS
dropout within 40dB of the track's own noise floor for longer than
~1s). That bar is what prevents the "silent hole" failure class from
regenerating.

================================================================
ONE-LINE SUMMARY
================================================================
Fix the two dominated human verbs under one principle, wire the
endgame sinks to the screen, assist the beginner smith, commit to the
Final Commission ending — and play the game yourself for one evening
before ruling on anything else.
